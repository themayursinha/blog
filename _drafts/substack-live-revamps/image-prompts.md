# Seeking Singularity live-revamp image prompt set

Generation mode: built-in OpenAI image generation

Use case taxonomy: `stylized-concept`

## Shared art direction

Create a wide Substack essay hero as a high-end literary-magazine editorial
illustration. Use layered ink and gouache on tactile paper: painterly,
restrained, intelligent, and slightly surreal. Keep the principal idea near
the center for safe thumbnail cropping and leave generous breathing room.

Use warm ivory, charcoal, muted indigo, oxidized gold, and restrained rust
red. Favor paper grain, dry brush, translucent layers, and fine ink lines.

Do not include readable text, logos, watermarks, glowing brains,
computer-interface screens, flowcharts, cyberpunk, neon, or glossy 3D.

## Asset concepts

### pile-of-hindu-texts-hero.png

A thoughtful person in a quiet study sits among uneven stacks of aged Hindu
books, scanned pages, and manuscript-like folios. The scattered material
gradually rises into an ordered constellation of connected pages and warm
points of light. Preserve tension between the physical pile and the emerging,
incomplete library. No readable script, deity imagery, sacred symbols used
decoratively, or temple spectacle.

### living-agent-ecosystem-hero.png

Distinct abstract machine-like inhabitants occupy a bounded terrarium or
architectural garden. They exchange packets of light, specialize into
different forms, and leave traces of shared memory. One form rests dormant
while a new form emerges. The system feels alive but governed, with a faint
undertone of risk. No human-faced robots or boxes-and-arrows diagram.

### metamorphosis-21st-century-hero.png

A solitary office worker sits at a small desk in a domestic room while their
human shadow subtly becomes an unfamiliar many-jointed silhouette. Family
figures remain at the edge, looking toward the desk without seeing the person.
Keep the worker human and make the transformation social and psychological,
not literal body horror. No exact Kafka illustration, giant insect, or gore.

### depths-of-consciousness-hero.png

A seated person in quiet profile holds two unresolved layers: grounded neural
branches in the head and translucent impressions of a lamp, cup, window, and
distant moon within a wider field of awareness. Neither measurable mechanism
nor lived experience dominates. Avoid religious iconography and psychedelic
cliches.

### quantum-finance-hero.png

Many faint possible paths flow across a sheet-like landscape toward one dark
mark: the market history that actually occurred. A human observer holds a
simple measuring frame that clarifies one view without magically causing the
market. Express probability, uncertainty, decision, and the danger of
mistaking metaphor for physical mechanism. No equations, tickers, currency
symbols, crypto coins, or glowing atoms.

### epicurean-paradox-hero.png

In a dim room, a coiled shape first appears threatening, then a narrow beam
reveals an ordinary rope. Beyond it, dark and light fields merge into one
continuous ground. Hold the reality of fear and suffering without
trivializing it. No deity, religious symbol, devil, scales, or gore.

### xai-truth-seeking-hero.png

A powerful abstract machine at an observatory-like table aims a narrow
instrument at a complicated night sky. It reveals one precise constellation
while other truths remain outside the frame. A human hand adjusts the frame,
showing that objectives, data, and power shape the search. No letter X,
branding, social-media motifs, or recognizable executive portrait.

### science-soul-hero.png

A human figure stands where two modes of understanding overlap: precise
natural patterns such as orbital arcs, tides, neural branches, and molecular
forms on one side; memory, grief, love, attention, and ordinary personal
objects on the other. Leave the overlap unresolved. No angel wings, departing
ghost, religious symbol, equations, glowing heart, or glowing brain.

### tesla-achievement-hero.png

An aging generic inventor sits in a modest hotel room facing several possible
technological futures: sunlight, power transmission, ocean motion, and
impossible machinery. Some paths are illuminated and grounded while others
dissolve into unfinished paper and smoke. The mood is elegiac and admiring
without becoming worshipful. No exact celebrity portrait, vehicle branding,
lightning spectacle, or glowing coil centerpiece.

### legacy-custody-hero.png

A quiet workshop or reading room after the maker has gone: one finished, visibly
used object rests on the work surface, and a second pair of generic unattributed
hands is already mid-task, continuing the work without ceremony. Show the
continuity of custody rather than a handover: tools worn smooth by use, a surface
holding the traces of many hands, one lamp still lit, the maker's chair empty and
out of the frame. No signature, no portrait, no bridge, no blueprint spread on a
table, no statue, no monument, no glowing structure.
